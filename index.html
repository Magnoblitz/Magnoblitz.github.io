Introduction

I am a 16 year old high school student with a passion for game and software development. I recently published my first game Ranger Rush, which I wrote entirely in x86 Assembly. There are many projects I have lined up for the future, and I hope you will stick around to see them through!

Coding

I have been coding for more than half my life, and have learned most of the major programming language including C, C++, C#, Javascript, Python, Lua, and HTML/CSS. But most recently I have been learning a new language: Assembly. The task was daunting but I dove right in, and I have really enjoyed coding in Assembly because it gives me complete control over the code I right, and I love the problem solving aspects of it.

As previously mentioned, my first ever game (Ranger Rush) was made in Assembly. It is a frogger-type game but with cowboys instead of frogs. It took me a bit over a month to make but mostly because I was learning Assembly as I made it. It is currenly in a "playable" state, but there are many bugs as well as many new features that I would like to implement. As I continuously update Ranger Rush, I will also work on another project that I have been planning in my head for years. I will add info about it here as I begin to work on it.
